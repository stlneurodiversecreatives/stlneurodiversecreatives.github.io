About STLNC

STLNC is a network for neurodivergent, alternative, and unconventional creatives from St. Louis to connect, collaborate, and build community together. We aim to create a space where creatives can share their work, discover opportunities, develop meaningful connections, and support one another. Our mission is to build a more accessible, connected, and supportive creative community in St. Louis. STLNC is rooted in mutual respect, radical inclusion, and mindful support, with the belief that creative communities are strongest when people have space to be themselves, share their perspectives, and grow alongside one another.

Our Future

Accessibility

Hosting and providing the resources to host inclusive, accessible creative events, exhibitions, workshops, gatherings, and other community experiences.

Community Lead Ideas

Creating opportunities for members to suggest, develop, and lead projects based on what the the creative community actually needs and wants.

Career and Professional Development

- Connecting creatives with resources, opportunities, mentorship, collaboration, professional development, and pathways toward sustainable creative careers.

Outreach and Education

Empowering the community to understand individual support needs, neurodiversity, disability, and alternative lifestyles while promoting the importance of inclusion and accountability. .

And More

STLNC is intended to grow alongside St. Louis. Our future will continue to be shaped by the ideas and needs of the people.
